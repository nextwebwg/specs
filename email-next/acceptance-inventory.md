# Whole-client acceptance coverage

A compatible client needs more than working encryption and attractive messages. It must preserve what was sent, put replies in the right place, protect the intended audience, survive interruptions and explain when something cannot proceed. Acceptance checks those behaviors together.

This inventory links the required behavior to its owning contracts. It is a plan for proving implementation support, not a claim that a complete client has passed. Existing experiments remain useful evidence for their recorded versions; they do not automatically cover the current agreement.

Current Encryption acceptance uses [mail-conversation-v2](suite-profile.md#current-client-baseline): fixed readable application, per-device interactions and independently ready actual deliveries. Earlier numbered experiments retain their evidence scope and cannot satisfy current dependencies by relabeling a pass. Role-specific acquisition/configuration claims select their matching current bindings.

## Choose the claim before choosing the tests

A report names the implementation, exact specification inputs and roles being claimed. A reading client, an Encryption client and a relay provider have different obligations. Optional modules can be omitted; required dependencies of a claimed module cannot.

:::: {.details summary="Scope, requirement records and evidence rules"}

Use the [conformance outcomes](conformance.md#report-observable-outcomes): pass, fail, unsupported and not tested. An omitted optional module is outside the declared claim, with an explicit scope disposition. Its unsupported-input and safe fallback behavior can still be required by a claimed role. An unsupported mandatory dependency or an unexecuted mandatory case prevents completion. A skipped case is not a pass or an implementation declaration.

For every applicable normative clause in an owning chapter, retain a requirement record with a stable local identifier, exact source path/section and source-byte digest, the clause or unambiguous bounded clause reference, role/module applicability and linked case identifiers. Include requirements stated in ordinary language and requirements incorporated from another selected contract. A keyword search or a chapter appearing in the table below cannot establish clause coverage. A changed source invalidates the old coverage disposition until the relevant differences are reviewed.

Each linked case specifies the actual inputs, preconditions, permitted authority, expected visible result, expected durable/remote effects and prohibited effects. Identify the observation that proves each expectation. For transactions include interruption points and retained state; for privacy include captured exposed bytes and authorized disclosure scope. A negative result needs evidence that the prohibited effect did not occur, not merely an error label. Separate a library primitive, wire decoder, trusted-input component, configured integration, deployed path, independent implementation exchange and manual usability/accessibility observation.

A report includes the requirement records, case results, tested source/fixture/dependency fingerprints, runtime/library/data versions and topology. A digest of one script does not pin its dependencies or the normative specification. Identify fixed configuration and trusted-input assumptions. Do not place secrets or real private correspondence in the report. Use the existing [acceptance manifest/report tools](adoption.md#acceptance-tools-and-interoperability-program) where they fit; manual and external observations retain equivalent traceability. Their historical manifest format is not a new Email/2 wire format or a complete current-suite runner.

The families below allocate the work; they do not replace any owning clause, finite schema enumeration or required acceptance case. A whole-client claim requires the clause ledger as well as the applicable family cases. No complete current-suite clause ledger/run is published here. The [design inventory](design-resolution.md) and [validation record](validation.md) retain remaining work and evidence limits.

## Test combinations and interruptions deliberately

:::: {.details summary="Finite module selection and cross-contract failure cases"}

For a current Encryption client, exercise the fixed twelve-kind readable application and each offered ordering with differing per-device interaction lists, including empty. Reject omitted/unknown/duplicate/unsorted names, reduced operation vocabularies and cross-mode evidence. Validate readable known optional data without unimplemented effects; a local interaction claim additionally requires its complete implementation. Earlier v1/v0 compatibility claims retain their exact thirty-two optional-block subsets and intersection/closure checks. Those earlier checks do not prove current assertion10 support.

For every required readable kind and implemented interaction, exercise accepted, unavailable and conflicted targets; wrong author/context/slot audience; duplicate and conflicting original bytes; reordered dependencies; and interruption at the durable namespace/effect boundary. Under current v2, deliver the same question to an Answers client and a simpler reader: one uses an authorized button, the other a permitted ordinary reply. Neither changes the common question or claims the other interaction executed. Current ordinary additions still require eligible native admission; older agreements use the exact compatible-stream migration, with no inherited history or rewritten uncertain send. Managed additions preserve their separately selected mode/governance and fresh-conversation migration boundary.

Across applicable families vary current/expired/uncertain time; unchanged renewal/changed binding/known revocation/identity repair; own/peer/provider account change; source loss/dependency arrival; local resource limits; and policy cancellation. Capture prepared, released, accepted and unavailable states separately. An unchanged source head is not a universal precondition and a high epoch is not branch authority.

At each boundary that consumes a package, installs authority/state, advances a mailbox cursor, releases bytes or applies an external effect, interrupt before and after the durable decision and after remote execution with a lost response. Reopen actual stores, replay exact inputs and inspect relevant remote/transport outcomes. Local and remote transactions need their owning uncertainty handling; a synthetic “crash happened” flag does not demonstrate it. Never include live or plaintext test credentials in the public evidence.

For mixed exposure combine authorized protected, ordinary, hosted and held copies, visible and private recipients, required/optional masking and verified/unverified policy. Inspect actual outer and inner data. Preserve each copy's outcome and contribution-wide disclosure; a successful private path cannot hide ordinary exposure elsewhere. Test old-client rendering and transformed real-provider carriage where those are claimed, rather than substituting raw MIME inspection.

## Example: a paired device sends without old history

:::: {.details summary="AC-25 example case: setup installed is not yet ready"}

This is a required case design, not a recorded pass. Use synthetic Alice, Bob and Dana accounts. Alice's existing administrator device has an unchanged verified Bob contact, an accepted private Alice/Dana conversation and a managed group. Alice's new device has independent keys and account login but no correspondence history. The managed group's required authority is temporarily unavailable.

| Step | Observation required |
| --- | --- |
| Approve pairing | Actual independent commitments match the new device, current account authority and exact enrollment. Swapping either request or return commitment installs no enrollment/setup. |
| Transfer configuration | Select configuration3 for current or mixed selections; configuration2 or configuration1 may separately cover their exact retained earlier-only selections, with enough synthetic public evidence to require multiple chunks. Bind the exact selected request/part/manifest version and reject cross-format substitution. Release exact signed encrypted parts/manifest, reorder them and restart midway. No prefix contact/context is live before complete authenticated private staging. Missing/substituted parts hold; exact retry retains bytes and original deadlines. |
| Reconcile | Bob's original verification provenance remains scoped and valid; the accepted private role pair and managed expected genesis are retained as own-device setup. No old messages, live group state or private package keys appear on the new device. In a separate variant, a conflicting preexisting Bob pin holds Bob’s selection rather than overwriting it; that variant cannot proceed to the successful Bob send below. |
| Prepare automatically | Current fresh signed setup/allocation and native ordinary preparation complete for Bob. Managed admission stays pending under its real authority. Neither an installed receipt nor cached setup alone marks the destination ready. |
| Send | Alice deliberately sends one new protected contribution to Bob. Observe actual submitted bytes, Bob's current-suite native admission/decryption and one accepted contribution after duplicate delivery. No history import, new human verification or test message is a prerequisite. |
| Return privately | Dana's separately enrolled device receives its own authorized configuration and sends a new private return contribution under the original Alice/Dana roles. Actual accepted encrypted From belongs to Dana; exposed mail and other copies disclose no private participant/proof. No unavailable old reply target is invented. |
| Change a dependency | Revoke the new device, change its account binding or invalidate the selected route between preparation and release. The affected new send stops at its serialized use-time boundary; already submitted/uncertain attempts retain their original outcomes. Bob's prior accepted contribution remains accepted. |

The report separates setup staging, per-record reconciliation, local readiness, transport acceptance and remote application acceptance. Independently developed clients and actual provider carriage are additional observations, not consequences of these local steps.

## What existing evidence establishes

The [validation record](validation.md#reusable-acceptance-reports) retains historical manifests and explicit unavailable cases. The [evidence guide](evidence/README.md) records newer component and configured integration work. For example, `test_suite_modules.py` exercises all thirty-two subsets and pairwise selection in both ordering modes, but its trusted decoded inputs do not verify device signatures, acquire packages or deliver a contribution. They supply useful inputs for individual families, but no family table row is itself a pass result and no current whole-client pass is claimed.

A current complete-bundle run still needs the clause-level ledger, integrated current encodings and all applicable family/module cases. Independent peers, real-provider carriage, native accessibility/usability evaluation and independent security review require their own observations. Public registration and standards adoption remain separate processes. These gaps stay visible even when specification compilation, a narrow decoder or a local loopback exchange passes.
