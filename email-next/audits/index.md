## Downloadable audit records

The [source inventory](source-inventory.json) and [section ledger](section-ledger.json) expose the recorded review status, with each section’s disposition and evidence link. They are review records, not a certification of completeness. Run `python3 email-next/evidence/audit_coverage.py` to check index uniqueness, source membership, published count statements, review-page links and section evidence links. A pending dependency review is not confused with a pending base-text review. The check does not establish substantive correctness or dependency closure.

## Missing update and replacement targets

RFC6750 and its listed updates RFC8996 and RFC9700 have recorded base reviews. RFC9325 and its listed updates RFC9852 and RFC10015 have recorded base reviews. RFC5280 update relationships: RFC8398 and RFC8399 have successors RFC9598 and RFC9549 respectively; current RFC9549 and RFC9598 have recorded reviews. The obsolete versions are historical references, excluded from the selected current profile; their metadata and errata were checked, but their base texts are not counted as fully reviewed. RFC6818, RFC9608, RFC9618, RFC9925 and RFC10007 have recorded reviews. RFC4648’s obsolete predecessor RFC3548 remains historical material outside the selected base review. Current TLS and authorization-profile dependencies remain open. This is not normative-reference or registry closure; those inventories remain unfinished. The [reference-candidate ledger](reference-candidates.md) exposes out-of-inventory RFC citations for applicability assessment; extraction does not add them automatically to the implementation requirements.

## Reviewed non-RFC dependencies

The [HTML language and direction audit](html-direction.md) records selected pinned source sections and their projection consequences. It is not counted as an RFC review or a complete HTML/CSS audit.

The [CSS declaration syntax audit](css-syntax.md) records selected pinned parsing paths and a known tokenizer difference. Property semantics and browser composition remain separate.

The [CSS cascade and inheritance audit](css-cascade.md) records inline precedence, descendant overrides and the limits of the current host.

The [selected font-property audit](css-fonts.md) separates inherited CSS semantics from the restricted sender vocabulary and client defaults. The [line-spacing audit](css-inline.md) records inherited ratios, font-dependent normal spacing and remaining decoration-overlap coverage.

The [Unicode rules for domain validation](idna-unicode-rules.md) review selected normalization, folding and property-data semantics. It does not select a runtime IDNA version or complete the property-data review.

The [CSS numbers and units audit](css-values.md) covers selected numeric and length semantics and a focused client-baseline browser check.

The [message-color audit](css-color.md) distinguishes permitted sender colors from client palettes and forced-color overrides.

The [text-wrapping audit](css-text.md) covers whitespace, permitted overflow wrapping and alignment, including the boundary between layout and retained text.
